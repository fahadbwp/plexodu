How Channel Audits & Earnings Are Calculated

All metrics represent authentic public data queried directly from the YouTube Data API v3.

analytics Subscriber & View Counts

YouTube rounds subscriber figures for channels exceeding 1,000 subscribers. Exact public view counts and publication dates are refreshed continuously.

payments Estimated Monthly Earnings

Computed using an industry-standard RPM range ($1.50 to $4.00 per 1,000 views) applied against recent publication velocity. Actual channel earnings depend on geography, niche, and monetization status.
