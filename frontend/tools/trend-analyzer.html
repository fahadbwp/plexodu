How YouTube Velocity & Momentum are Calculated

PLEXUDO aggregates publicly visible video metrics and channel publication frequencies through public APIs and telemetry. To ensure transparent insights, our heuristic algorithms operate under the following standards:

speed View Velocity (views/hr)

Requires at least two comparable public observation timestamps. Velocity is computed as the differential quotient of view count divided by the elapsed delta time. When fewer than 2 snapshots exist, velocity is marked as Initial Calibration.

hub Plexudo Momentum Index (0–100)

A weighted heuristic combining 24h upload acceleration in the parent category, comment-to-view ratios, and category relative performance benchmarks.

forum Comment Sentiment Sampling

Public sentiment parses the top 100 most relevant public comments via lightweight NLP classifiers. Moderated, spam, or withheld comments are excluded from calculations.

lock_open Public & Indexable Access

All reports on this public tier require no authentication. Data reflects publicly available YouTube metrics and complies strictly with the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.
