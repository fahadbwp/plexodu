Public Intelligence & FAQ

How YouTube Velocity & Momentum Are Calculated

Plexudo aggregates publicly visible video metrics and publication timestamps directly through the official YouTube Data API v3. To ensure transparent insights, our heuristic algorithms operate under the following engineering standards:

speed View Velocity (views/hr)

Video rankings estimate lifetime-average views per hour from total public views and upload age. Observed changes require at least two comparable saved snapshots; upload-age buckets are not a historical velocity curve.

hub Plexudo Momentum Index (0–100)

A relative percentile rank of estimated lifetime views per hour within the current returned video sample. It is not measured acceleration, a channel baseline comparison, or a virality probability.

forum Comment Sentiment Sampling

Sentiment estimates use TextBlob polarity and Urdu/Hindi lexicon rules on up to 50 public comments requested for the leading video. This is a limited returned comment sample, not the whole audience or a verified moderation audit.

lock_open Public & Anonymous Access

All tools on this public tier require zero login, cookies, or account registration. Data strictly reflects publicly available YouTube metrics and complies fully with YouTube API Terms of Service.
