Public Intelligence & FAQ

How YouTube Velocity & Momentum Are Calculated

Plexudo aggregates publicly visible video metrics and publication timestamps directly through the official YouTube Data API v3. To ensure transparent insights, our heuristic algorithms operate under the following engineering standards:

speed View Velocity (views/hr)

Requires at least two comparable public observation timestamps. Velocity is computed as the differential quotient of view count divided by the elapsed delta time. When fewer than 2 snapshots exist, velocity is marked as Initial Calibration.

hub Plexudo Momentum Index (0–100)

A weighted heuristic combining 24h upload acceleration in the parent category, comment-to-view ratios, and deviation from the channel's 90-day baseline performance.

forum Comment Sentiment Sampling

Public sentiment parses the top 100 most relevant public comments via lightweight local transformer classifiers. Moderated, spam, or withheld comments are excluded from calculations.

lock_open Public & Anonymous Access

All tools on this public tier require zero login, cookies, or account registration. Data strictly reflects publicly available YouTube metrics and complies fully with YouTube API Terms of Service.
