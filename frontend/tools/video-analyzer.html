How Video Diagnostics & Virality Scores Work

Plexudo extracts and parses public video metadata, view counts, and audience responses strictly from official YouTube Data API endpoints.

Heuristic derived from upload elapsed time versus accumulated view velocity, like-to-view proportions, and discussion velocity across public comments.

Measures snippet clarity in YouTube mobile feeds, front-loaded title keywords, description structure, timestamp chapters, and tag spam compliance.

Evaluates the emotional tone of top public comments to reveal whether high view counts are driven by genuine appreciation, skepticism, or controversy.
